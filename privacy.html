Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

Google Drive access is used solely to upload, list, update and delete the owner's backup files in the owner's own Drive.

No data obtained through Google APIs is collected, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising or any other purpose.

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own machine. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
